I'm heading to ACL this weekend. And so are several others in ITAO. 

It's very exciting. 
ACL, the Annual Meeting of the Association for Computational Linguistics, is the top conference each year for work on computational linguistics (CL) and natural language processing (NLP). 
I'd call CL/NLP my computer science academic home (with information systems being my business academic home), so it's very exciting to see Mendoza's ITAO department being so well-represented this year. 

Here is a list of papers/posters that ITAO folks will be presenting at the ACL main conference and co-located workshops. 
Congrats everyone!
